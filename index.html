How we work

Not an agency, not a label

Virellox books nobody, signs nobody and takes no cut of anything. Be careful with anyone who charges for exposure, guaranteed slots or playlist placement — that industry is full of it.

We also keep clear of rights. Play and remix what you own or have licensed; we don't clear samples, we don't provide legal advice, and if you're planning a commercial release with somebody else's material, that's a conversation for a lawyer.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do I need CDJs?

No. A basic controller teaches everything that matters, and we'll cover the differences so club gear isn't a shock. We sell nothing and recommend by need.

Is using sync cheating?

It's a tool. We teach beatmatching by ear anyway, because it builds the timing awareness that makes everything else easier — then use sync if you like.

Can you get me gigs?

No. We're not an agency and take no commission. We coach the skills and the approach; bookings come from your local scene.

Which DAW should I use?

The one you already have. Switching is the most common form of procrastination in production, and we'll say so bluntly.

Can I release a remix?

Only with the rights to do so. We explain how it generally works and we're not lawyers — clearance is a legal matter, not a coaching one.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

Want honest notes on a mix?

Thirty minutes of your mix, twenty minutes of feedback, free.
